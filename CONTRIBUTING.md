# Contributing to Paste

Paste is developed and maintained by a single person. Please keep that in mind when opening issues or pull requests.

## Pull requests

I am currently **not accepting pull requests**. If you have an idea or a fix, please open an issue first and describe it. If I think it fits the project, I will let you know how to proceed.

## Issues

Bug reports and feature ideas are welcome.

- Search existing issues first.
- Describe what you did, what you expected and what actually happened.
- Include your macOS version and the app version, and steps to reproduce if possible.
- One issue per problem.

## Our policy on AI

**Using AI tools is fine. AI-generated submissions are not.**

You may use AI tools to help you understand a problem, find a bug or translate and polish text you wrote yourself. What matters is that a human writes the issue, has verified its content and takes responsibility for it.

Not allowed:

- Issues (or pull requests, if they are ever opened) created by an AI agent, bot or automation.
- Text or code pasted from an AI tool without being reviewed and verified by you.
- Bug reports with invented or unverified reproduction steps, logs or version numbers.
- Bulk or drive-by submissions produced with automated tooling.

If AI tools played a significant role in your submission, a short note is appreciated but not required.

Submissions that appear to be AI-generated and unreviewed may be closed without further discussion. Repeated violations can lead to being blocked from the repository.
